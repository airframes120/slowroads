NOV 23 / DAY 1

Irvine, CA → El Paso

Approx. 799 miles · 12 hours driving · 150 minutes breaks · 90 minutes delay allowance

CA-241 / CA-91 / CA-60 → I-10 E via Phoenix, Tucson and Las Cruces

Suggested breaks

  • Indio: 25-minute breakfast and dog walk after about 2 hours.
  • Quartzsite: 25-minute fuel, water and driver rotation.
  • Casa Grande: 35-minute lunch and longer leash walk.
  • Willcox: 25-minute fuel and driver change.
  • Deming: 40-minute meal and dog walk before the final stretch.

Traffic & safety

Leave before the Inland Empire commute. Check Phoenix I-10 versus the signed Loop 202 bypass; El Paso may be busy on arrival. Bypass miles and hotel access vary.

This is a 14.5-hour elapsed day before delays. A 3 AM start requires an early bedtime the night before. Desert winds, dust and darkness can make progress slower. Stop if either driver becomes drowsy.

For your husky

Use maintained travel plazas for leashed walks and water every 2–3 hours or sooner. Inspect paws for cactus spines and hot or sharp pavement.

Lodging / fallback

West or northwest El Paso near I-10 for an easier arrival. Confirm actual hotel location: an east-side property changes today’s and tomorrow’s mileage. Prioritize sleep over crossing the city.

NOV 24 / DAY 2

El Paso, TX → Houston

Approx. 745 miles · 11.25 hours driving · 135 minutes breaks · 90 minutes delay allowance

I-10 E via Van Horn, Fort Stockton, Junction, San Antonio and Katy

Suggested breaks

  • Van Horn: 20-minute fuel, dog walk and driver change.
  • Fort Stockton: 30-minute breakfast and longer walk.
  • Ozona / Sonora area: 25-minute fuel and water stop; choose a safe business off I-10.
  • Junction: 30-minute meal, walk and driver rotation.
  • Seguin: 30-minute comfort break before the Houston approach; take extra stops as needed.

Traffic & safety

San Antonio around midday and Houston in the afternoon are the main congestion risks. Check conditions near Katy. East Houston or Baytown helps tomorrow only if reaching it is safe.

Before using the 4 AM start, protect 7–8 hours of sleep opportunity after check-in, meals and dog care. If Monday runs late, move departure later. Texas deer, trucks and long service gaps call for conservative pacing.

For your husky

Let the husky decompress after check-in. Combine fuel with meaningful walks, and keep a spare water bowl available to the passenger.

Lodging / fallback

East Houston / Baytown is the preferred departure side if both drivers are alert. West Houston is a valid fatigue stop but adds distance on Wednesday. Verify policies directly and book flexible terms.

NOV 25 / DAY 3

Houston, TX → Sarasota

Approx. 1,032 miles · 15 hours driving · 180 minutes breaks · 120 minutes delay allowance

I-10 E → I-12 E through Baton Rouge / Hammond → I-10 E through Mobile and Tallahassee → I-75 S via Lake City

Suggested breaks

  • Lake Charles: 25-minute breakfast, fuel and leash walk.
  • Hammond: 25-minute walk and driver change after Baton Rouge.
  • Gulfport area: 30-minute meal and longer dog walk.
  • Pensacola area: 25-minute fuel, walk and driver rotation; reassess fatigue.
  • Tallahassee: 30-minute meal and decision point: stay overnight if tired or delayed.
  • Lake City: 20-minute walk and driver change before I-75 south.
  • Wesley Chapel area: 25-minute final fuel, water and comfort break before Sarasota.

Traffic & safety

Wednesday before Thanksgiving: Baton Rouge bridge approaches, Mobile tunnel and Tampa-area I-75 can all delay the trip. Use I-12 to avoid New Orleans. Buffer is an allowance, not a traffic forecast.

About 18 hours elapsed before delays, with substantial night driving. A 3 AM start only works after sufficient sleep; postpone it otherwise. If tired in the Panhandle, add a hotel night and finish Thursday. Missing a target is preferable to driving drowsy.

For your husky

Water and relief every 2–3 hours or sooner; add stops as needed. Avoid marsh edges and standing water. Sarasota beach access for dogs is restricted: verify designated dog areas before visiting.

Lodging / fallback

No third night is in the baseline budget. Keep an extra-night reserve for Tallahassee or an earlier safe stop. From Tallahassee allow about 327 miles / 5.25 hours driving plus breaks via I-10 and I-75; Thursday arrival before 6 PM dinner is still only a target.
